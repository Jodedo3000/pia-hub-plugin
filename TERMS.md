## 2. Who it is for

The plugin is an **educational tool for psychotherapists in training** (in Germany: PiA under the PsychThG in force before 2020) and for others learning psychotherapeutic methodology. It is not intended for patients, clients or the general public seeking help.

## 3. What it is not

The plugin does **not** provide healthcare. Specifically it does not:

- give medical, psychological or therapeutic advice to laypeople,
- make diagnoses or recommend treatment for any individual,
- replace supervision, personal therapy, formal training or professional judgement,
- constitute legal advice on training regulations.

Its patient-simulation skill deliberately does **not** generate scenarios involving acute suicidality or danger to others, because the safety behaviour of a third-party host model cannot be verified by us.

## 4. Crisis situations

This plugin is not a crisis service. If you or someone else is in acute danger, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line. In Germany: emergency services on 112, Telefonseelsorge on 0800 111 0 111 or 0800 111 0 222.

## 5. Accuracy

The skills describe methodology. The actual answers are produced by the AI model you run them in, and that model can be wrong. Questions the plugin generates are explicitly marked as not editorially reviewed, unlike the curated question bank on the PiA Hub itself. Verify anything you rely on against primary sources, guidelines and your supervisor.
